

## Exercise 05: Gaussian Fundamental Quantities

Derive the following three groups of Gaussian fundamental quantities for the **Spherical Mercator projection**:

1. Quantities on the reference surface:

   [
   e,\quad f,\quad g
   ]

2. Quantities associated with the map-projection transformation:

   [
   \bar{E},\quad \bar{F},\quad \bar{G}
   ]

3. Quantities on the projected map plane:

   [
   E,\quad F,\quad G
   ]

All mathematical symbols must be used **strictly and consistently**.

The symbols

[
e,\ f,\ g,\quad
\bar{E},\ \bar{F},\ \bar{G},\quad
E,\ F,\ G
]

represent different quantities and **must not be interchanged**.

In the final section of the solution, provide a clear comparison of the three groups:

[
(e,f,g),\qquad
(\bar{E},\bar{F},\bar{G}),\qquad
(E,F,G)
]

Explain their definitions, roles, mathematical relationships, and differences.
